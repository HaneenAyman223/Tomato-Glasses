    Why they stay put, and why the fit matters.

    A child only sees clearly when the pupil sits in the middle of the lens. If the frame slides, they look through the edge and the eyes do not get the help the lenses are meant to give. Tomato frames adjust so the lens stays centred.

    Non-slip nose pad and tips
    Stay put when they jump or run.
